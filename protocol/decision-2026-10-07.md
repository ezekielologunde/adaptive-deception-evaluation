# Next project decision: deception policy selection under persistent adaptation

Status: feasibility investigation. No novelty clearance, confirmatory results or publication-ready manuscript yet.

## Decision

Proceed with the existing adaptive-deception project, narrowly focused on whether offline policy selection predicts subsequent protected-asset outcomes. Preserve the completed authorization study separately. Do not start another authorization variant or a generic RL honeypot.

Working question: when a simulated attacker retains knowledge across encounters, can a defender select a deployment policy from historical interaction logs without systematically overstating its future protection? Compare estimates against independent online rollouts under the same declared deployment history.

The potential contribution is a reproducible evaluation of policy-selection reliability, including a decision to abstain when the evidence is inadequate. It is not the discovery that attackers adapt or that nonstationarity biases evaluation. Those ideas already have substantial prior coverage. A cyber application alone does not establish novelty.

## Distinguish three estimands

1. One encounter with a newly initialized attacker.
2. Deploy a new defender now, retaining the attacker's actual historical knowledge.
3. Counterfactual lifetime deployment: the attacker would have learned under that defender all along.

They are different questions. Historical defender action probabilities do not by themselves make all three identifiable. Within-encounter adaptation is compatible with ordinary trajectory importance sampling if the history and transition law are appropriately modeled; changing the learning/reset law between collection and evaluation violates that comparison. Do not call every adaptive setting non-identifiable.

## Go/no-go criteria

Proceed to main collection only when: (a) closest full papers and artifacts are compared; (b) a concrete contribution beyond applying OPEN is articulated; (c) identical-history counterfactual controls pass; (d) a source-audited simulator smoke test runs; (e) action probabilities and raw protected-asset outcomes are independently verifiable; and (f) realistic compute is measured.

Stop or redesign if the result is only the expected failure of a knowingly misspecified stationary estimator, depends on privileged attacker state unavailable to the proposed method, or duplicates an existing benchmark. Preserve useful replication evidence without labeling it a new discovery.
